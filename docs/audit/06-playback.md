# Preview playback and scrubbing reliability (PreviewEngine, TimelinePreview, PreviewBox, ProxyEngine)

The preview architecture is sound (timeline time is the authority, A/B roll parity matches the exporter, no rebuilds for grade/caption changes) but the multi-clip path was never given the lookahead that the audio path has, so every cut and every transition start begins with a cold seek that stalls the clock and briefly shows a stale frame. The biggest fidelity defect is that surfaces are bare TextureViews filling a box shaped by the FIRST clip, so any clip or overlay of a different aspect is stretched in the preview while the export letterboxes it. Several smaller but traceable defects: redraw() walks the paused frame backwards 1 ms per call (and can show the frame before a trim in-point), the 2-second unstick reload can loop forever on a slow EXACT seek and reloads every surface, overlay players have no error recovery or unstick, speed ramps restart the audio stretcher every tick, the end of the edit parks one frame past the out point, playback continues in the background, and proxies only ever cover the first source. Versus CapCut the notable gaps are fullscreen preview, overlay/per-clip audio, pause-on-scrub and frame stepping.

## ux · medium · S — Scrubbing while playing does not pause: the finger and the transport fight over the playhead

**Detail:** engine.seekTo keeps `playing` true and scrubTo never clears isPlaying, so dragging the ruler mid-play keeps the clock advancing between pointer events; each tick writes positionMs back into state.playheadMs while the drag writes its own value, so the playhead jitters and playback resumes from wherever the finger last was. Fix: pause on drag start (TimelineEditor already reports onScrubbingChange), optionally resume on release.

**CapCut:** CapCut pauses playback the moment the timeline is touched.

**Evidence:** PreviewEngine.kt:571-581 (seekTo leaves playing untouched), 661 and TimelinePreview.kt:184 (position pushed to state every tick); TimelineEditor.kt:600-601 (scrubbing state available).

## gap · medium · M — Overlay (PiP) audio is always muted, and video clips have no per-clip volume

**Detail:** Overlay players are created with volume = 0f and the export also strips overlay audio (setRemoveAudio(clip.isOverlay ...)); the base players use one global originalVolume for every clip. A reaction-cam or screen-recording overlay therefore cannot be heard, and one base clip cannot be quieter than another. Fix: per-clip volume on video clips (Clip.volume already exists and is used for audio clips), applied in syncSurface by setting player.volume for the active clip, and in the export via buildAudioProcessors per clip.

**CapCut:** Every CapCut clip, base or overlay, has its own volume and mute; overlay audio plays and mixes.

## bug · high · M — No lookahead for video surfaces: every cut stalls the clock and flashes a stale frame

**Detail:** The next roll's player is only loaded/seeked once the playhead is already inside the incoming clip. composeBase() finds clipB with covers() and syncSurface() then does setMediaItem+prepare+seekTo (new file) or seekTo (same file) at the very moment the cut is due. In the same tick the incoming SurfaceDraw is visible=true, so the TextureView shows whatever it last rendered (the last frame of the clip two cuts back, or black) until the EXACT seek lands; meanwhile the clock hands over to the incoming clip, which is BUFFERING, so `stalled` holds the playhead. Result: a hitch and a wrong-frame flash at every hard cut, and the first part of every dissolve/slide/wipe blends against a stale frame. The audio path already solves this with PREROLL_MS (seek the next cue 2 s early); video has no equivalent. Fix: when the clock clip has <~1 s left, resolve the next base clip and park the idle roll's player on its first frame (and likewise for upcoming overlay clips); hide a surface until its player reports STATE_READY after the seek (or listen for onRenderedFirstFrame).

**CapCut:** CapCut cuts and transitions are gapless; the next clip's first frame is already decoded.

**Evidence:** PreviewEngine.kt:371-372 (index-parity roll assignment: consecutive clips alternate players); 696-699 + 856-857 (composeBase resolves only clips covering the current time; no lookahead); 805-831 (syncSurface loads/seeks the incoming roll on the first tick it is covered; EXACT seek from 251); 722-729 (visible=true returned in the same tick) and TimelinePreview.kt:283-290 (alpha set directly from draw.visible, no readiness gate); PreviewEngine.kt:945-948 (attachSurface never clears the TextureView, so the stale frame persists); 715-720 (clock handed to incoming clip in the cut tick) then 627-646 (`stalled` = clock player STATE_BUFFERING holds positionMs on subsequent ticks); 612-619 and 867-884 with 1020 (audio pre-roll via primeAudio/PREROLL_MS; no video counterpart; grep confirms no onRenderedFirstFrame listener). ## bug · high · S — redraw() drifts the paused frame backwards 1 ms per call and can show the frame before the trim in-point

**Detail:** redraw() seeks each player to `currentPosition - 1`. The next redraw starts from that new position, so every call moves the parked picture back one more millisecond. It is called on every pause(), on every preview resize (panel open/close), and once per tick while a grade/caption/effects value change is pending, which during a brightness or look-strength drag is every 33 ms. A 4-second drag is ~120 redraws = ~4 frames earlier than the playhead says. syncSurface's correction only fires when the drift exceeds VIDEO_RESYNC_MS (120 ms), so smaller drift persists and on play the clock (derived from the player) jumps back. When parked exactly on a clip's start, `sourceIn - 1` is the last frame BEFORE the trim in-point, which is the one frame a trim is meant to hide. Fix: redraw by seeking to the true target `clip.sourceAt(positionMs)` and alternate ±1 ms around it, never around currentPosition.

**CapCut:** CapCut re-renders the parked frame at the playhead position; the frame never moves unless the playhead does.

**Evidence:** PreviewEngine.kt:594-601 (redraw seeks to currentPosition - 1, never to clip.sourceAt(positionMs)); TimelinePreview.kt:149,160-165 (grade is in editSignature, so each slider value re-runs setTimeline) -> PreviewEngine.kt:361 (pendingRedraw on grade change), 407-410 (crop), 487 (background), 560 (pause) -> 671-674 (one redraw per 33 ms tick, TICK at TimelinePreview.kt:403); TimelinePreview.kt:174-178 (redraw 150 ms after each picture resize). Correction only at PreviewEngine.kt:844-848 when |currentPosition - wanted| > VIDEO_RESYNC_MS (120, line 1017) and not within CORRECTION_GAP_MS (400, line 1030), so drift is bounded at ~120 ms but persists below that; play() 542-553 does no seek and the clock at 650 reads clockPlayer.currentPosition, so the playhead jumps back by the drift on play. Drift resets on engine.seekTo (578 clears activeClip -> 828-831 re-seeks to wanted). Correction to the claim: a single `sourceIn - 1` seek shows the same frame, not the frame before the in-point (ExoPlayer drops buffers with pts below the seek target and renders the first frame at or after it, which is what the comment at 591-592 relies on); the pre-in-point frame appears only after accumulated drift crosses a frame boundary, roughly 33 redraws (about a second of continuous slider drag) while parked on a clip start. Suggested fix stands: redraw around the true target from clip.sourceAt(positionMs) per surface, alternating +/-1 ms, rather than around currentPosition.
