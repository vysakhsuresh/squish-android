Good morning, and welcome to the market. Today we are going to cook a simple dinner from what we find here. First, the vegetables. These tomatoes are red and soft, so they are ready to eat today. The onions are small and sweet. We will need garlic, ginger and a little green chilli for heat. The rice comes from a farm two hours away, and the farmer says this year was a good year because the rain came on time.
Now we walk to the fish stall. The fish was caught early this morning, before the sun came up. Look at the eyes: they are clear, which means the fish is fresh. We will take two pieces and ask for them to be cleaned. The man at the stall has worked here for thirty years, and he knows every family that buys from him.
Back at home, we wash the rice three times and leave it in water for twenty minutes. Meanwhile we cut the onions and fry them slowly in oil until they turn gold. Then the garlic and ginger go in, and the kitchen begins to smell wonderful. The tomatoes follow, with a spoon of salt and a pinch of turmeric. We let it cook until the tomatoes break down into a thick sauce.
The fish goes into the sauce for eight minutes, no more, so that it stays tender. While it cooks, the rice boils and then rests with the lid on. Finally we add fresh coriander and a squeeze of lemon. Dinner is ready. It took less than an hour, it cost very little, and everything on the plate came from people we met today. Thank you for watching, and see you next week.
